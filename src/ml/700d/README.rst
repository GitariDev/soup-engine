Soup Engine
===========

Film looks, in the camera.

Put the film stocks (the ``.fs`` files) in ``ML/FILM`` on the card and
pick one under Shoot > Soup Engine. Every photo you take after that gets
a second JPEG saved beside the raw, ``FLM_1234.JPG``, with the look
already on it. The raw file is never modified, and the camera will not
switch itself off while frames are still waiting.

Sizes: Quarter (1296x864) takes about 11 seconds and is the one to use
for a quick frame to share. Half (2592x1728) takes about 40 seconds and
Full (18 MP) about three minutes. Keep shooting while it works; frames
queue up.

Always eject the card from the computer before removing it. A card
pulled while still mounted can leave the camera unable to start with it
until the card is cleanly ejected again.
